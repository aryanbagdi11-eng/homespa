How Much Does a Home
Massage Cost in Dubai?

Published 1 July 2026 · Updated 7 August 2026

Home massage pricing in Dubai varies more than salon pricing, since it depends on the therapist's travel, session length and specialty — but there are some general patterns worth knowing before you book.

What affects the price

Three factors matter most: the massage type (deep tissue and sports massage often cost more than a basic relaxation session), the session length, and how far the therapist has to travel within Dubai. Some therapists also charge slightly more for evening or weekend slots.

What's usually included

Quoted prices typically include the therapist's travel, all equipment (table, oils, towels), and the session itself. It's worth confirming this upfront, since a small number of therapists charge travel separately for areas further from their base.

Comparing to salon pricing

Home massage is often priced similarly to or slightly above equivalent salon treatments, reflecting the convenience and travel time saved. For many guests, the time saved on commuting more than offsets the difference.

Getting an accurate quote

Because pricing depends on therapist, area and session type, we don't publish a single fixed rate — when you request a match, the confirmed price comes directly from the therapist before you book, so there's no surprise at the end of the session.
