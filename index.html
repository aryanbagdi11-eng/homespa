Common Questions

Before you
book.

Does the therapist bring their own equipment?

Yes — massage tables, facial equipment, and all tools and products are brought by the therapist. You just need a bit of floor or room space.

Is this a real spa business?

No — we're an independent matching service. We connect you with licensed, independently-run mobile therapists; treatments happen at your location, not ours.

Which areas of Dubai do you cover?

All of Dubai — from Downtown and Marina to the outer residential communities. Coverage depends on therapist availability in your specific area.

How much notice do I need to give?

Same-day booking is often possible depending on therapist availability, though booking a day ahead gives more choice of time slots.

Is it free to use?

Yes, matching is completely free. You pay the therapist's normal service rate directly.
